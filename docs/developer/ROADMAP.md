### Source-bound verification

The earlier combined local checkpoint passed 1,728 Node tests with one opt-in
skip on each of Node 22.15.0 and 24.22.2, plus all 76 Chromium cases, syntax,
native-helper integrity, documentation, types, design tokens and bundle parity.
Later scoped Claude closure/idle handling passed 121 focused checks and 70
independent runtime/controller checks on each Node version. These results retain
their exact source boundaries; they are not a substitute for the final
candidate's required combined checks and package manifest.

Earlier public CI and detached smoke apply only to their earlier reviewed
sources. Current local verification is separate while public updates are held.
Record the final candidate revision, artifact identities, checks and material
limits in source-bound release evidence.

### Native evidence and limits

Retained native paths cover each client, one Copilot/Codex pair, exact queued
approvals, pending and owned-running cancellation with surviving work, ordinary
report review, owned session import/export and fresh-controller saved-report
reopen after Xenoflux source copies become unavailable. Vendor histories stay
untouched. Successful tasks/checks remain distinct from imported history with
unknown completion, settings or verification.

Capture remains partial where the native interfaces omit evidence. Host ancestry
is sampled, escaped descendants remain unverified, and missing phase, turn,
effort, timing or usage observations stay unknown. One pair does not establish
repeatability or general quality. Privacy evidence combines retained nonempty
native-body omission with normal-UI synthetic positive inclusion/redaction
controls; it is not a universal scan of credentials or arbitrary private prose.

Detailed originals and grades remain private under `local/4.0.0/`, including G's
`local-first/retained-core-status.md` and its evidence matrix. Chat mappings,
account metadata, operational grants and prompt accounting stay outside public
source. Historical receipts are never relabeled as newer qualification.
